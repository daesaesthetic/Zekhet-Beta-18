==================================================

==================================================
ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKS
==================================================

Achievements should automatically unlock when their requirements are met.

Examples:

Creating a profile → First Record

Obtaining 10 titles → Courtier

Discovering 25 lore entries → Archivist

Receiving a curse → Marked

Completing a contract → Oathbound

Discovering secret lore → Secret achievement

Obtaining secret titles → Secret achievement

Do not require users to manually claim achievements.

==================================================
UNLOCK NOTIFICATION
==================================================

When an achievement is unlocked, send a polished Zekhet-themed notification.

Example:

⛤ ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED ⛤

🏺 FIRST RECORD

"The archives now recognize your name."

Rarity: Common

Do not make notifications excessively long.

Rare and Secret achievements should have more dramatic wording.
