==================================================
1. PASSPORT CONCEPT
==================================================

The Passport should represent everything a user has accomplished within Zekhet.

The existing /profile represents:

"Who you are."

The Passport should represent:

"What you have done."

The Passport should feel like an ancient official document issued by Zekhet.

It should be prestigious, collectible, and visually distinct from /profile.

The Passport should NOT simply duplicate the entire profile.

It should summarize the user's history and accomplishments.
