==================================================

Add approximately 8–12 secret Passport Stamps.

Secret stamps should NOT reveal:

- Their name
- Their description
- Their requirement
- Their rarity

before unlocking.

In the Passport they should appear as:

🔒 UNKNOWN RECORD

or an equivalent Zekhet-themed placeholder.

After unlocking, reveal:

- Name
- Description
- Rarity
- Unlock date

Examples of possible concepts:

THE UNRECORDED

ZEKHET REMEMBERS

THE FORBIDDEN PAGE

BEYOND THE ARCHIVE

==================================================
4. SECRET REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

Secret stamps should rely on interesting combinations of existing systems.

Examples:

Own a particular title AND discover a particular lore entry.

Complete a contract after obtaining a particular title.

Discover several rare lore entries.

Receive multiple different curses.

Complete the tutorial and reach a certain progression level.

Own titles from multiple rarity levels.

Unlock several achievements across different categories.

Obtain a rare item and a specific achievement.

Reach a progression milestone without owning a particular category.

Perform an unusual but reasonable sequence of existing actions.

Do NOT create requirements that are impossible to discover.

Do NOT require arbitrary command spam.

Do NOT require thousands of repetitions.

The goal is:

"How did you get that?"

not:

"I guess I need to grind this for 40 hours."
